Website purchases are processed by our online reseller Paddle.com, which is the Merchant of Record for these orders. You can request a refund within 14 days of purchase, for any reason.

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method. The time it takes to appear depends on your bank or payment provider. After a refund, Pro Access is removed from your account.

We cannot issue refunds for purchases made through Apple or Google, because they process those payments. Please request your refund from them:
